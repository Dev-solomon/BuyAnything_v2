Product Research

How to research high-demand products before you decide to sell them.

A high-demand product is not simply the item with the most views. Good product research asks whether interest is recent, whether people appear ready to buy, whether the product can be fulfilled reliably, and whether the economics leave enough room to market it.

Use several demand signals

Strong research combines independent sources. Recent search behavior can show rising curiosity. Social platforms can reveal demonstrations and repeated buyer questions. Marketplace reviews can show whether people continue purchasing and what they value. Competitor advertising can indicate that businesses are willing to pay to reach buyers. Supplier data can help confirm that a product and its variants are actually available.

Demand momentum

Is interest rising recently, stable, or falling? A current trend matters more than an old viral spike.

Buyer intent

Are people only watching content, or are they asking price, shipping, comparison, and availability questions?

Margin room

After product, shipping, transaction, platform, and advertising costs, is there enough room for the test to make economic sense?

Supplier fit

Are the exact variants, images, fulfillment locations, and delivery options available from the connected supplier?

Creative potential

Can the product be demonstrated quickly and clearly in a video, image, comparison, or problem-and-solution format?

Advertising suitability

Can it be marketed honestly without restricted claims, regulated categories, counterfeit branding, or policy-heavy promises?

Avoid choosing products from one metric

Large view counts can be misleading. A product may receive attention because it is unusual, funny, controversial, or inexpensive, yet still convert poorly. Likewise, a product with lower public attention can perform well when the audience has a specific need and the offer is clear.

Research should lead to a test, not a guarantee

No research process can guarantee a winning product. The purpose is to improve the odds of choosing a sensible test. Start with controlled spend or organic traffic, watch the full funnel from click to checkout, and use real conversion data to decide whether to continue, adjust the offer, change creative, or select another product.
