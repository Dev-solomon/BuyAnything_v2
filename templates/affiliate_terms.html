AFFILIATE AGREEMENT

BuyAnything Affiliate Terms.

Effective October 1, 2026. These terms govern participation in the BuyAnything affiliate platform.

1. Affiliate role

Affiliates use their BuyAnything dashboard to research products, approve a product for their individual storefront, and market that storefront. BuyAnything provides the storefront, checkout infrastructure, product sourcing workflow, and fulfillment integration. Affiliates are independent participants and are not employees, agents, partners, or legal representatives of BuyAnything.

2. Product approval & storefront

Each affiliate may approve products made available through the platform research and sourcing workflow. An approved product is tied to that affiliate's account and storefront. Affiliates may promote only products and claims that are lawful, accurate, and consistent with the product information supplied by BuyAnything. Affiliates must not invent discounts, reviews, guarantees, health claims, delivery guarantees, or product capabilities.

3. Earnings and 15% platform fee

The dashboard displays the affiliate's recorded earnings for qualifying paid orders. For each unit, BuyAnything first excludes the configured $16 shipping component from the retail selling price and deducts the supplier product cost. The remaining amount is the commissionable product margin. BuyAnything retains 15% of that commissionable product margin as its platform fee, and the affiliate receives the remaining 85%.

Affiliate earnings = (Selling price − $16 shipping component − supplier product cost) × 85% × quantity

The $16 shipping component is not itself reduced by the 15% platform fee and is not treated as affiliate earnings. If the commissionable margin is zero or negative, no affiliate earnings accrue for that unit. Dashboard amounts may be adjusted for refunds, chargebacks, cancellations, fraud, duplicate transactions, fulfillment failures, or calculation corrections.

4. Qualifying sales & fulfillment

A sale qualifies when payment is successfully received and attributed to the affiliate storefront. Orders may be fulfilled through CJdropshipping or another approved provider. Affiliates can view orders attributed to their account in their fulfillment center. Estimated customer delivery is 3–7 days, but affiliates must present this as an estimate rather than a guarantee.

5. Payouts

Affiliates are entitled to the payable earnings shown on their dashboard after applicable adjustments. BuyAnything may reasonably hold or reverse amounts connected to refunds, disputes, chargebacks, suspected fraud, or orders that do not qualify. Payout method, identity/tax verification, minimum payout thresholds, and payout schedule may be communicated through the platform or directly to the affiliate as operations require.

6. Marketing standards

Affiliates are responsible for their advertising activity and must follow applicable advertising, consumer-protection, privacy, intellectual-property, platform, and disclosure rules. Where required, affiliate or commercial relationships must be clearly disclosed. Spam, deceptive marketing, impersonation, counterfeit promotion, unlawful content, and misleading claims are prohibited.

7. Returns and customer service

Customer purchases are subject to BuyAnything's Shipping & Returns Policy, including the 7-day return-request window after delivery. Refunds and chargebacks can reduce or reverse affiliate earnings associated with the affected order.

8. Account integrity & termination

Affiliates must keep account credentials secure and provide accurate account information. BuyAnything may suspend or terminate participation for fraud, abuse, unlawful activity, material breach of these terms, or conduct that creates material risk to customers or the platform. Amounts legitimately earned before termination remain subject to refunds, disputes, and other valid adjustments.

9. Changes & contact

BuyAnything may update these terms as the program evolves. Material changes should be communicated through the platform or affiliate contact information. Continued participation after an effective update may require renewed acceptance. Questions may be sent to contact@buyanything.sale.
