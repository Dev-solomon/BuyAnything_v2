OUR POLICIES

Clear Rules - Simple Shopping.

These policies apply to purchases made through BuyAnything marketer storefronts. Last updated October 1, 2026.

Shipping & Delivery

Our estimated delivery window is 3–10 days after an order enters fulfillment. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed delivery date. Carrier delays, destination, customs, weather, address issues, inventory availability, and supplier processing can affect actual delivery time. Tracking information is provided when available.

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address. Contact us promptly if you notice an address error; changes cannot be guaranteed after fulfillment begins.

7-day return policy

You may request an eligible return within 7 calendar days after the order is marked delivered. Return requests made after that 7-day window are not accepted except where applicable law requires otherwise.

To qualify, the item should be unused, unworn, unwashed, and in substantially the same condition received, with original packaging and accessories where applicable. For hygiene, safety, customization, perishability, or other lawful reasons, some products may be non-returnable; any product-specific restriction should be disclosed before purchase.

Damaged, incorrect or defective items

Contact us as soon as reasonably possible and within the 7-day return window. Include the order number and clear photos or video showing the issue. We will review the request and, when validated, arrange the appropriate replacement, return, or refund.

Refunds

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method after the return is received and inspected when a return is required. Bank and card processing times vary. Original shipping charges and return shipping costs are non-refundable unless the item was defective, damaged, incorrect, or applicable law requires otherwise.

Cancellations

Cancellation requests should be submitted immediately in 24hrs time. Because fulfillments are usually automated, an order cannot be guaranteed cancellable once supplier processing has an reached irreversible state. If cancellation is no longer possible, the return policy applies after delivery.

Payments & Privacy

Payments are processed securely through Stripe. BuyAnything does not store full card numbers. Order and contact information may be used as necessary to process payment, fulfill the order, provide customer support, prevent fraudulent activity, and comply with legal obligations. Fulfillment information may be shared with our suppliers and delivery carriers as necessary to complete the order.

Contact

For order, return, or policy questions, contact contact@buyanything.sale and include your order number when applicable.
